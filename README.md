# Privacy Policy — Shibomo

**Last Updated: October 28, 2026**

## 1. General

Shibomo (the “App”) is a puzzle game published under the package name `com.shibomo.app`. This Privacy Policy explains how the App processes data.

## 2. Data We Collect

- **Local game progress, settings, and preferences:** Stored on your device and not sent to our servers through a user account.
- **Usage analytics:** Information such as level starts, level completions, and screen views, collected through Firebase Analytics.
- **Crash and error reports:** Collected through Firebase Crashlytics.
- **Advertising ID and device- or advertising-related technical data:** Processed by Google AdMob to display advertisements.

## 3. How We Use Data

We use data to improve analytics and app stability, display occasional advertisements, and enhance the overall user experience.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following Google services:

- **Firebase Analytics**
- **Firebase Crashlytics**
- **Google AdMob**

These services are subject to Google's respective privacy policies and terms.

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google AdMob Privacy Information: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## 5. Data Retention

Local data remains on your device. You can reset your game progress through the App's Settings menu.

Analytics and advertising data are retained in accordance with the applicable data retention policies of the relevant Google services.

## 6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You can limit ad personalization through your device settings and reset your local game progress within the App.

Since the App does not provide user account creation, there is no centralized account deletion process.
